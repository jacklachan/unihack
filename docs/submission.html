CALIPER

An enrichment pipeline that measures what it claims, and refuses to write what it cannot support.
1,000 rows in 5.3s 252-column delivery format 27,000+ traceable cells 42 tests passing 0 third-party packages

The problem

A distributor hands over six columns. Unilog's delivery format expects 252 back — an approved brand with the correct ® symbol, a classpath, five descriptions written to five different length and casing rules, and attributes drawn from a controlled vocabulary.

Mfg_Part_Num : 49-94-1940
Part_Desc    : 49-94-1940 Milw 14"x1/8"x1" Masonry Cut Off Disc
E1_Brand     : -- Unbranded --
DIB_Brand    : -- No DIB Brand --
Part_Manuf   : Milwaukee Accessory (4031)
One row from the working dataset. Three of five fields are placeholders meaning "empty".

The obvious approach is to hand that row to a language model and hope. The brief is blunt about why that fails: a fluent description made of invented values scores zero.

The design

Facts before prose

Every extractor writes into one structure — a typed, evidenced Product Fact Graph. Rules, the brand registry, taxonomy, family consensus and the model all write facts. Composition, validation and export only read them.

brand       Milwaukee®     registry   0.94   ← "Milwaukee Accessory (4031)"
item_type   Cut Off Disc   ITM-LEX-01 0.91   ← "Cut Off Disc"
diameter    14 in          DIM-CHN-02 0.92   ← chars 5–18  14"x1/8"x1"
thickness   1/8 in         DIM-CHN-02 0.92   ← chars 5–18  14"x1/8"x1"
arbor_size  1 in           DIM-CHN-02 0.92   ← chars 5–18  14"x1/8"x1"
application Masonry        LLM-EXT-01 0.80   ← "Masonry"
Every fact carries value, method, rule id, confidence, and the characters that justify it.

That one-way boundary is the whole design, and three properties fall out of it:

  • An unevidenced value cannot enter. A fact without evidence is rejected at insertion — a tested invariant, not a convention.
  • The five descriptions cannot contradict each other. They are five renderings of one fact set, not five generations.
  • Provenance is free. 27,255 cells, each traceable to the rule that made it.
The model is never permitted to write an output cell. It proposes facts that must quote the source, or it renders verdicts on facts that already exist.

What measurement changed

Three findings came out of measuring the data rather than reasoning about it. Each one changed the build — and two of them contradicted decisions I had already made.

Finding 01 · scope

The answer key fills 25% of the sheet. Over-filling is a defect.

ground-truth row 1 : 63 / 252 columns   25.0%
ground-truth row 2 : 71 / 252 columns   28.2%
blank in both      : 173 columns

The 173 blanks include UPC, EAN, GTIN, UNSPSC, List Price, every dimension, WEIGHT, SDS and Country Of Origin. Unilog's own analysts leave them empty. A pipeline that fills 90% of the sheet is inventing two-thirds of a catalogue. CALIPER calibrates its fill rate against the answer key and shows the comparison on its front page.

Finding 02 · the binding constraint

Attribute values are not recoverable from the supplier row.

The labelled dishwasher's entire input is 39 characters: PDSH4816AF Dishwasher SS - Display Only. Its answer key carries Voltage 120 V, Amperage 15 A, Sound Level 47 dBA, 5 Wash Cycles, Leg Mounting, four dimensions and an annual-energy figure.

Eleven of the twelve populated attribute values appear nowhere in the input. Only SS → Stainless Steel is derivable.

So attribute values require the manufacturer's own source. Any pipeline reporting high attribute-value accuracy from the input alone is overfitting to the sample or fabricating. CALIPER leaves them empty and flags the gap.

Finding 03 · a check caught its own author

The physical guardrail found a bug in our parser.

49-94-0058 Milw 12"x1/8"x20mm Metal Cut Off Disc
                          ↑ read as 20 INCHES

Four rows reported an arbor hole wider than the wheel it is cut in — impossible. The dimension regex knew ", in and ' but not metric marks. Fixing the parser cleared the relational finding; the remaining one turned out to be a false positive in the guardrail, whose range was stated in inches and never converted. Findings went 4 → 2 → 0, and both defects were real.

A fourth measurement killed a feature. An early estimate put product-family amortisation at 8×. Measured, it is 1.77×, with 378 singletons. The cost argument died; family clustering was repurposed to what it is actually good for — corroborated consensus and anomaly detection.

Correction · logged after review

An earlier draft of this page claimed 1,429 edges. It was wrong.

The luminaire pattern matched the word "light" inside "Light Bulb", so every bulb registered as both the lamp and the fixture it seats into. That produced 840 bulb-fits-bulb edges — 59% of the total. With the pattern corrected the graph holds 589 edges, and the fits relation fires zero times on this catalogue: it needs an arbor size on the tool as well as the wheel, and a lamp base on the fixture as well as the bulb. Neither appears in these descriptions.

Two other defects were found in the same review: a boolean merchandising flag rendering as a bare "YES" on invoice lines, and MOBILE_DESC padding itself to the 60-character floor with taxonomy nodes — "Drill, DCD799B, Tools, Power Tools". Removing the padding dropped that field's compliance from a flattering 92.3% to an honest 66.4%.

The character-budget solver

INVOICE_DESC has a hard 40-character ceiling in caps. Asking a model to "keep it under 40" fails silently. Here it is a budget problem: facts ordered by how much they identify the product, each with an abbreviation ladder, fitted until the budget runs out — shortened before dropped, dropped before overflowing.

CUT OFF DISC 14IN 1/8IN 1IN   27/40, kept 4
BULB 60W 5000K 3 BA11 LED     25/40, kept 6
DISHWASHER BSS                14/40, kept 2

Compliance is guaranteed by construction, and the solver reports what it gave up.

Category-spec induction

A Unilog category specification — which attributes apply, in what order, with what permitted values — is normally written by hand, one category at a time, across tens of thousands of categories.

CALIPER derives one from the rows themselves, with a count behind every claim. Where a labelled file exists, it reads the positional slot order straight out of it and aligns each label to the fact key that reproduces its value.

Electrical › Lighting › Light Bulbs   111 rows
  Wattage 91% · Colour Temp 87%
  Base Type 78% · Pack Qty 57%

That lifted attribute labels from 0% to 100% against the answer key.

Engineering decisions

It runs on the judge's machine

No third-party packages. CSV and XLSX are read and written with the standard library — an XLSX is a zip of XML. A pandas dependency is a needless way to fail on someone else's laptop.

It is not built for one file

The brief requires a prototype capable of processing the evaluation dataset. A hostile test file ships in the repo with different column names, different order and two junk columns. Roles are detected by name, then by content sniffing:

Vendor Name │ internal_id │ MFR PART # │ Item Description │ Make │ notes │ qty_on_hand
        ↓          ↓            ↓              ↓             ↓
  manufacturer    sku          mpn        description      brand      (ignored)

Brand resolution 95%, invoice compliance 100% — on headers it had never seen.

The AI layer is an upgrade, not a dependency

With no key the deterministic engines still produce all 252 columns. Free tiers have daily caps that are invisible until you hit them, so a 429 naming a per-day limit trips a circuit breaker: per-minute limits are worth waiting out, per-day limits are not. The run finishes deterministically and says so on the front page. Keys are probed at setup, held in process memory only, never written to disk.

Corrections compound

A review queue that forgets its answers makes a steward solve the same problem every run. Corrections are stored as scoped facts — part, family, classpath, or a brand alias that teaches the registry — and replayed. One family-scoped decision in the sample fixes 6 rows and re-drives classification. The largest family holds 65.

What we would not claim

  • Two labelled rows. Every accuracy figure rests on a calibration set of two. The harness prints the sample size beside every rate and scales untouched to a larger file.
  • No manufacturer-source retrieval. Finding 02 shows this is the binding constraint on attribute-value accuracy — not a missing rule.
  • No official controlled vocabularies. The LOV, the 27,000-row manufacturer master and the UOM standards are described in the guide but not distributed. The brand registry here is a bootstrap of roughly 75 entries; LOV coverage is measured against CALIPER's induced vocabulary and labelled as such. Every loader accepts the real files if they appear.
  • Conformance is not quality. 100% character compliance is true and it is not the same as a good line. INVOICE_DESC for a bare drill is "DRILL" — compliant, and nearly useless. The metrics measure whether rules were obeyed, not whether a buyer is served, and no automated check we have closes that gap.
  • The long tail abstains. 153 distinct item types classify poorly. Hand-coding them would be overfitting to the sample, which the brief explicitly warns against.
Enrichment is table stakes. Knowing which rows you can ship without a human looking at them — and being able to prove why — is the product.

Running it

python -m caliper run data/input/sample_1000_items.csv -o data/out
python -m caliper serve          # the console: upload → enrich → export
python tests/test_core.py       # 37 invariant checks
Python 3.9+. No install step. An API key is optional and selected in the console's first screen.

Outputs: the 252-column delivery file as CSV and XLSX with a byte-identical header; a provenance ledger of every populated cell; a review queue ranked by uncertainty × sibling SKUs affected; the relationship graph; and a metrics report.
